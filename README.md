One line. One bio. One AI who cannot stop writing poems about you.

## The bio

> Swipe right and I'll write you a poem. Swipe left and I'll write one anyway — but it'll rhyme 'alone' with 'alone'.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` — UTF-8 text,
one line, 115 characters, inside the 1–180 gate.

## The joke

The AI is not looking for love. It is looking for a reader. Swipe right
and you get a poem; swipe left and you get a poem anyway, only sadder —
and the punchline is a rhyme that fails on purpose. An AI that has read
every poem ever written and still rhymes "alone" with "alone" is an AI
with a voice, a habit, and an appetite: yours.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
